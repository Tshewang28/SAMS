SAMS Games & Sports Assessment Access Fix

Change:
- Games & Sports is removed from the Assessment Dashboard for appointed assessors.
- Principal remains Discipline-only.
- Vice Principal/non-class teacher assessors can assess Classroom, Assembly, SUPW and Discipline, but not Games & Sports.
- Games & Sports must be entered only from Classes & Students by the Class Teacher assigned to the selected class/section.
- A hard save guard also rejects Games & Sports if someone tries to invoke it directly on the assessment dashboard.

Main changed file:
js/assessment-dashboard.js
